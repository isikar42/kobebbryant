How it works

Three steps that keep your webshop and your team ready for ransomware and phishing.

  1. 1

    Lock it down (once)

    We check logins and two-step verification, backups, plugins and email settings, and fix what's open.

  2. 2

    Keep watching (every week)

    We scan for known weaknesses, watch for changes and suspicious logins, and check that your backups work.

  3. 3

    Your team (all year)

    Trainings, practice phishing emails and an emergency plan everyone knows.

Every month you get one report: what we checked, what we fixed and what you need to do.

What we don't do

We're not a firewall, we don't replace your hosting security and we don't do recovery after an attack. If something serious happens, we advise on the first steps and refer you to a specialist.
