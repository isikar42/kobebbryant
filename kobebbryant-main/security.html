How we protect your data

  • Only the access it needs. Each agent gets its own account with the minimum permissions.
  • A person approves what matters. Payments, outgoing emails and anything unusual wait for sign-off.
  • Every step is logged. You can always see what the agent did and why.
  • EU-hosted. Your data stays in the EU and is never used to train AI models.
